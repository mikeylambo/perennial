# Discard to Ascend / Perennial: Onboarding

The first run teaches everything without text boxes (rule 8). Each idea gets one room, in the order a player can feel it. Button prompts are cut-paper glyphs that appear once and fade. The first run is **the Foothill**, a short tutorial face of Emberfell (one year, half length), then the player is dropped into a real run.

## 0. Boot to first control (under 20 seconds)

A pop-up book opens. A letter folds itself into Folio on the first page and drops onto an autumn slope. The player is in control before the page finishes settling. No menu before the first room; the menu appears after the Foothill.

## 1. Room F1: run, jump, crumple

A long downhill. Leaves blow past. A jump glyph appears at the first gap. At the first steep slope a slide glyph appears: Folio crumples and rolls, bouncing two Leaf Mites. **Lesson:** going down is fast and fun.

## 2. Room F2: flatten and dart

A low root tunnel (flatten glyph), then a wide drop with an updraft (hold-jump glyph). Folio folds into a dart. **Lesson:** your body is paper, and that is your toolkit.

## 3. Room F3: the first keepsake

A shrine holds the **Ember Blade**. Picking it up fills slot 1 and shows its verb glyph. Three Leaf Mites and one Acorn Jay test it. **Lesson:** an item is a verb.

## 4. Room F4: a full pack

Two more finds: the **Fish Hook** (a swing over a gap) and a **Bent Nail** (scrap, no verb). The pack shows three slots, full. **Lesson:** you can hold three things.

## 5. Room F5: the first plot

Two stakes with red flags: one on the low path, one on a ledge above it. On approach, the bury glyph appears. (Tutorial rooms use two sockets; real rooms have two or three, and the choice decides where the landmark rises in spring.) A fourth find (Tallow Lantern) sits just past the plot on a ledge you can only reach after making space. The game never says "bury the nail." Most players bury the nail; some bury the blade. Both are fine. **Lesson:** burying makes space, and the plot remembers.

## 6. Room F6: the winter band

Snow starts. The palette cools. A second plot sits here with a faint **two-leaf mark** on its stake (winter plots grow twice). A Frost Moth makes Folio damp: the dart stops working. **Lesson:** winter is heavier, and winter plots are worth more.

## 7. Room F7: the Drift Wolf (short version)

A small guardian fight with one phase. It is designed to be clearly harder if you buried the blade, but still winnable with the hook and lantern or bare. **Lesson:** the guardian tests what you kept.

## 8. The turn

The wolf falls. The snow melts in one long camera pull up the face. Each plot pops up in turn, piece by piece, with the bloom chord. A nail becomes a stump. A blade becomes the Sunblade tree. This is the game's signature moment, played in full the first time and shortened later.

## 9. Rooms F6 and F5 in spring: climb what you gave

The same rooms in spring. A bare route exists but is visibly long and thorny. The landmark route is right there. At the Sunblade's top, a bloom glows: the harvest glyph appears, and **Sunblade (T2)** goes into the pack. **Lesson:** what you buried is the way up and the weapon.

## 10. The Hearth, then the Fallow

At the top, the Hearth mends creases and shows a pop-up plate of this year's landmarks. Then Folio walks down into the grey Fallow; one small patch turns green. The menu appears for the first time: **Climb** (start a real run on Emberfell).

## 11. Introduced later, one beat each

| Idea | Where it first appears |
|---|---|
| Replanting | Year 2 autumn: the first plot glows when you carry a harvested keepsake |
| Seed-charms | First Hearth in a real run |
| Elites | Year 1 winter band of a real run (Snow Golem) |
| Theft (Acorn Jay, White Stoat) | Year 1 of a real run |
| Landmark threats (Gnaw Vole, Weed King) | Year 2 climb |
| The Magpie King | First Year 3 summit |
| Hard Winters | After the first summit |

## 12. Unlock gating *(default)*

- After the Foothill: Emberfell runs.
- First summit (either ending): the Glasswater, Hard Winters 1, Daily Mountain.
- First Glasswater summit: the Long White.
- Any summit at Hard Winters 5: Endless Years.
- Keepsakes unlock in order through Fallow growth (one per two completed years).

## 13. Assists (settings, never shame)

Slower game speed (70/85%), extra creases (+2), guardian telegraph extension, auto-glide on hold, a "show bare route" overlay on the climb, and colorblind palettes for each season (snow caps and plots also differ by shape and pattern, never only by color).
